1.  PURPOSE This policy explains the leave process for employees of
    Example Technologies.

2.  ELIGIBILITY All full-time employees are covered by this policy after
    completing their joining formalities. Interns and contractors follow
    the terms of their individual agreements.

3.  LEAVE TYPES

-   Casual Leave (CL): 8 days per calendar year.
-   Sick Leave (SL): 8 days per calendar year.
-   Earned Leave (EL): 18 days per calendar year, credited monthly at
    1.5 days.
-   Parental leave: handled according to applicable law and the
    company’s separate parental leave procedure.

4.  APPLYING FOR LEAVE Employees should submit leave requests through
    the HR portal.

-   Planned leave of 1-2 working days: request at least 3 working days
    in advance.
-   Planned leave of 3 or more working days: request at least 7 working
    days in advance.
-   Sick leave: inform the manager as soon as reasonably possible. Leave
    is approved only after the manager or authorised approver confirms
    it.

5.  PUBLIC HOLIDAYS AND WEEKENDS Company-declared public holidays are
    not deducted from an employee’s leave balance. Weekly offs are
    handled according to the employee’s work schedule.

6.  CARRY FORWARD Unused Earned Leave may be carried forward up to a
    maximum balance of 30 days. Casual Leave and Sick Leave do not carry
    forward.

7.  CONTACT For questions about leave balances or this policy, contact
    the HR team at hr@example.com.
