HR POLICY DOCUMENT Document: Business Expense and Reimbursement Policy
Version: 1.0 Effective date: 1 January 2026 Company: Example
Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (Fictional)

IMPORTANT This is fictional sample content created for software testing.
It is not legal advice and does not represent a real company’s policy.

1.  PURPOSE This policy explains how employees can claim reimbursement
    for eligible business expenses.

2.  ELIGIBLE EXPENSES Subject to prior approval where required, eligible
    expenses may include:

-   Business travel by approved transport.
-   Hotel accommodation for approved business trips.
-   Meals during eligible business travel.
-   Approved work-related supplies and software.

Personal shopping, traffic fines, minibar charges, and expenses
unrelated to business are not reimbursable.

3.  APPROVAL Employees must obtain manager approval before making any
    non-routine or high-value purchase. Travel bookings must follow the
    company’s travel approval process.

4.  SUBMISSION DEADLINE Submit reimbursement claims within 30 calendar
    days after the expense date or completion of the business trip,
    whichever is later.

5.  REQUIRED DOCUMENTS Each claim must include:

-   Date and amount of the expense.
-   Business purpose.
-   Original or digital itemised receipt.
-   Manager approval, where required.
-   Travel details, if the expense relates to a business trip.

6.  PROCESSING TIME HR or Finance will review complete claims. Approved
    claims are normally processed within 10 business days, subject to
    payroll and payment cycles. Incomplete claims may be returned for
    clarification.

7.  QUESTIONS For reimbursement questions, contact finance@example.com.
